## Real-World Applications of MediResQ

1. **Emergency Response and Life Saving:** Helps save lives by quickly connecting accident victims and patients experiencing critical medical conditions, such as
 heart attacks, with nearby hospitals and ambulances. Reduces delays when every second matters.

2.**Smart Appointment and Queue Management:** Allows people with minor health issues, such as colds, coughs and fever, to book appointments online instead of waiting
in long hospital queues. Saves time for both patients and healthcare providers.

3. **Advanced Ambulance and Traffic Coordination:** Supports faster ambulance movement through real-time GPS tracking, traffic-aware route planning and coordination
 with hospitals, helping ambulances reach patients and hospitals more efficiently.

4.**Digital Medical Records and Online Doctor Consultation:** Maintains digital medical records that can be accessed by authorized patients and hospitals. 
Provides interactive doctor-patient communication through chat, WhatsApp and video consultations, supported by an AI assistant that helps users understand
general medical information and navigate healthcare services.

5. **Medical Awareness and Specialist Events:** Organizes online and offline awareness sessions led by medical specialists to educate people about diseases
6. , symptoms, prevention, precautions and treatment options. Encourages better health awareness and early medical consultation.
